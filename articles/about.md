

I was always fascinated with films, animation, comic books and video games since I was a kid, so it's natural that I ended up doing something related to that for a living.

After working for a few years in Rio de Janeiro, where I grew up, I spent about 8 years doing Animation at Blur Studio in Venice, California, where I started doing Animation and Layout, moved on to supervising animation and then directed some TV commercials in my last two years there.

In 2010, I joined Pixar Animation Studio to help create the feature film **Brave**, and I've been there ever since, working in the Camera and Staging department. My job is to create a kind of prototype of the film, often using temporary dialogue and music, and with very rough-looking animation and lighting, since the point is not how it looks, but how it plays.

We experiment with camera placement, lenses and shot choreography (character blocking and staging). Then we collaborate with the editors and the director, so we can refine the storytelling and find solutions that feel believable from a narrative perspective and work well in a three dimensional space.

If you browse the Work section in this website you can see what I've been up to in the last 20 years or so.
Cheers!
