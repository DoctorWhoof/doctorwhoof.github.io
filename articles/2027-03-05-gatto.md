When the pandemic hit in 2020 I was a Layout lead on [Luca](/#project-2021-06-18-luca), a job that I did mostly working from home while taking care of a 5 year old. It was a lot, and I asked my manager to not be in lead positions again for a while, at least until my kid was a little older.

[image-right](media/thumbnails/gatto.jpg)

I kept contributing as a regular artist on projects I absolutely loved working on, like [Win or Lose](/#project-2025-02-19-win-or-lose) and [Elio](/#project-2025-07-28-elio), until I joined Gatto in late 2024 as a lead again.

I was intrigued by the gorgeous artwork and inspired by a great screening of the work in progress of the film at the point, with storyboards only.

There's not much else I can say until after the film is released, of course, other than I'm really happy to be a part of this one, it's a Pixar film like no other one before.
