When the pandemic hit in 2020 I was a Layout lead on Luca, a job that I did mostly working from home while taking care of a 5 year old. It was a lot, and I asked my manager to not be in lead positions again for a while, at least until my kid was a little older.

I was still contributing as a regular artist on projects I absolutely loved working on, like Win or Lose and Elio, but late in 2024 I joined Gatto as a lead again, intrigued by the gorgeous artwork and inspired by a great screening of the work in progress of the film at the point, with storyboards only.

There's not much else I can say until after the film is released, of course, other than I'm really happy to be a part of this one, it's a Pixar film like no other one before.
